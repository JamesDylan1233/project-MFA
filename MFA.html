    Why this matters in a distributed system

    • Central IdP, stateless tokens. Services never see the password — they validate a short-lived bearer token, so a compromise of one node doesn't leak credentials.
    • Clock skew. TOTP depends on synchronized clocks across nodes; this verifier tolerates ±1 time step (RFC 6238 §5.2).
    • Replay protection. A code is bound to its time step and rejected if reused, defeating interception of a valid OTP.
    • Rate limiting. Lockout after repeated failures blunts credential-stuffing across the fleet.
    • Recovery paths. Backup codes and device trust are deliberate trade-offs — each widens the attack surface in exchange for availability.
